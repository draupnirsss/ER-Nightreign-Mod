## About

A fan-made mod that brings *Elden Ring Nightreign* (NR) into *Elden Ring* (ER), aiming for **the best Nightreign experience inside ER**:

- **Nightfarers** – each hero with their moveset, passive, skill, ultimate, relics, weapons and outfits, behaving like in NR.
- **Nightlords** – the bosses with their arenas, music, sky and fight scripts. Normal and Everdark versions are merged into one fight.

The **Tarnished stays pure Elden Ring**: Nightreign rules only apply while you play a Nightfarer.

## First release – 0.1.0 (in progress)

The first version is small on purpose, so that everything in it plays right:

| | What you get |
|---|---|
| 🗡️ **Wylder** (Nightfarer) | NR moveset, the *Sixth Sense* passive, the *Claw Shot* skill and the *Onslaught Stake* ultimate, his NR weapons and outfit |
| 🏃 **Nightreign movement** | Nightreign-style movement for Nightfarers, based on Neirox's *Nightreign Movement* and polished |
| 🐺 **Gladius, Beast of Night** (Nightlord) | Normal and Everdark versions merged into one fight, on its Nightreign arena, with its music |

More Nightfarers and Nightlords follow in later releases (see the roadmap below).

## Requirements (for players)

- *Elden Ring* on Steam, kept at the game version stated on each release.
- [Mod Engine 3](https://github.com/garyttierney/me3).
- **Offline only.** The mod runs the game without Easy Anti-Cheat; never go online with it. It uses its own save file, so your normal save is untouched.
- This is a fan tribute: please support FromSoftware by owning *Elden Ring Nightreign*.
